    For the teacher: where Hoang needs correcting or adding to
    • Hoang's margin note on p. 189 defines average fixed cost as costs "that do not change with the level of output". That is total fixed cost; average fixed cost falls as output rises, as Hoang's own p. 193 shows.
    • The rule is printed as "Profit maximization = MC = MR" (p. 189) without saying marginal cost must be rising where it meets marginal revenue. The lab's table and graph both show why that matters.
    • On p. 194 the loss-making firm "will still produce where MC = MR". That holds only while price covers average variable cost. Hoang never states the shut-down rule: shut down occurs 0 times in the book.
    • Vocabulary: Hoang's abnormal or supernormal profit is the AP exam's positive economic profit, and Hoang's average cost (AC) is average total cost (ATC). Accounting profit occurs 0 times in Hoang, and the AP name profit-maximizing rule occurs 0 times too.
    • In Hoang's chapter the topic is HL only. In AP Microeconomics it is core content for every student.
    • The answer booklet for Question 14.3(c) says the price rises "from $8 to $12"; the figure's prices are $8 and $16, and its own areas use $16.
